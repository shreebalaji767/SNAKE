HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

Control the snake and collect the glowing food. Every food item increases your score and makes the snake longer.

AVOID

Do not hit the outside walls and do not run the snake into its own body.

COMPUTER

Use W A S D or the Arrow Keys to move. Press SPACE to pause or resume. Press ESC to open the menu.

PHONE / TABLET

Use the on-screen D-pad or swipe directly across the game board.

SCORING

Each food item gives 10 points and increases the snake length by one segment.
